Glaze v20: reply stuck in Reasoning + new story prompt helpers
==============================================================
Not compiled (no Flutter SDK here). Run: flutter analyze && flutter test
New tests: test/reasoning_salvage_test.dart, test/prompt_helpers_test.dart (group added)

1. Reply ends up inside "Reasoning", message empty
   Cause: when the model/provider puts the whole answer in the reasoning channel
   (reasoning_content, or an unclosed/odd <think> block), the app saved an empty
   message and marked it "all reasoning".
   Fix: lib/core/llm/reasoning_salvage.dart, called in stream_generation_service.dart
   right before the message is saved, only when the visible reply is EMPTY:
     a) text after a closing </think>-style tag becomes the reply;
     b) text after a "Final response:" / "Reply:" label becomes the reply;
     c) if the whole reasoning reads like roleplay (has *actions* or "dialogue",
        no "The user wants / I should" style analysis) it becomes the reply.
   Real thinking is left alone (an empty reply you can regenerate beats showing
   analysis as the story). Your screenshot's reasoning is plain third-person
   analysis without quotes/actions, so it would NOT be moved automatically; if
   that happens often with a model, the usual causes are: the reply hit the max
   response length while the model was still thinking (raise Max Response
   tokens), or the model needs the reasoning effort lowered/turned off.

2. New Prompt Helpers (API settings > Prompt Helpers and chat Tools; global)
   - Stay In Character: no talking about instructions, the character sheet or
     rules ("she always has an attitude", "is allowed to ..."), no author notes
     or OOC unless you write (OOC) first.
   - Stay On Story: every line must follow from the scene and your last message;
     no random filler events/objects/tangents, no repeated lines.
   - Limited Knowledge: characters only know what they saw/heard/were told in
     the chat, so they cannot instantly know unshared things about you.
   Sent with the other helpers as the last system block. Strings added to
   en.json and ru.json. Existing saved helper choices keep working.

Limits: prompt helpers reduce these problems but cannot guarantee them; they
work best with a model that follows system instructions well.
