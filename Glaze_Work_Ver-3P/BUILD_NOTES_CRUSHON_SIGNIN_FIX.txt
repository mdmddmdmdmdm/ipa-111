CrushOn sign-in fixes (false "Looks signed in" + Google "browser may not be secure")
====================================================================================

Problems
--------
1. Status banner said "Looks signed in…" as soon as https://crushon.ai finished
   loading, because onLoadStop treated any non-auth CrushOn URL as signed-in.
   Google OAuth still showed the account form while the banner claimed success.

2. Google OAuth returned: "Couldn't sign you in — This browser or app may not
   be secure." The login WebView used a generic Android Chrome UA that Google
   still classifies as an embedded WebView.

Fixes (crushon_login_sheet.dart)
--------------------------------
• User-Agent switched to the same Safari iOS string SpicyChat uses (no "wv"
  marker). Google OAuth accepts this the same way the SpicyChat sheet does.

• WebView settings aligned with spicyChatWebViewSettings: shared cookies,
  hybrid composition, wide viewport, mobile content mode, etc.

• Status logic:
  - On Google / Discord / Apple / CrushOn auth paths → "Finish signing in…"
  - On CrushOn host → only "Looks signed in" when real auth cookies are present
    (session/token/auth/jwt/user/…), not merely cf_clearance.
  - onLoadStart clears the false positive as soon as OAuth starts.

How to verify
-------------
1. Open Third-party providers → CrushOn → Sign in.
2. Banner should stay on the sign-in prompt until you actually complete login.
3. Tap "Sign in with Google" — Google should accept the flow (no "may not be
   secure" screen) when the device/network allows OAuth.
4. After landing back on CrushOn, banner becomes "Looks signed in…"; Done
   harvests cookies into CrushOnWebSession.
